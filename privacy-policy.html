Privacy Policy for PassApp

Last updated: October 17, 2026 · App version: 1.0.0

PassApp is a password manager application designed to securely store credentials on the user's device. This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed and how it is handled.

1. Data Controller

Developer: Applicados
Contact: infogooweb@gmail.com

2. Information Stored by the Application

  • The application does not require user registration.
  • We do not request names, email addresses, or other personal identification information.
  • Passwords and credentials entered by the user are stored exclusively on the user's device.
  • Stored information is protected using local encryption mechanisms.

3. Local Storage

All information managed by the application remains on the user's device.

  • No data is transmitted to external servers.
  • No cloud synchronization is performed.
  • We do not have access to the passwords stored in the application.
  • The user is solely responsible for safeguarding their master password and backups.

4. Biometric Authentication

The application may offer biometric authentication (fingerprint or facial recognition) to simplify access.

  • Biometric data is managed exclusively by the device's operating system.
  • The application does not collect, store, or transmit biometric information.
  • Biometric verification is performed locally through the APIs provided by Android or iOS.

5. Backups

If the user uses the backup or export features:

  • Backup files are generated only on the user's device.
  • The application does not store copies of exported files.
  • The protection and safekeeping of backup files are the responsibility of the user.

6. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect personally identifiable information.
  • We do not collect email addresses.
  • We do not collect passwords on external servers.
  • We do not collect financial information.
  • We do not collect location data.
  • We do not use cookies.
  • We do not track user activity.

7. Advertising and Third-Party Services

The application does not display advertisements.

  • It does not use Google AdMob.
  • It does not share data with advertising networks.
  • It does not sell information to third parties.

8. Analytics

The application does not use analytics or tracking tools.

  • We do not collect usage statistics.
  • We do not use Firebase Analytics.
  • We do not record user behavior events.

9. Security

The application implements reasonable measures to protect information stored locally. However, no system can guarantee absolute security.

The user is responsible for:

  • Keeping their master password secure.
  • Protecting physical access to their device.
  • Safeguarding any backups they create.

10. Children's Privacy

The application is not specifically directed to children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect improvements, legal requirements, or application changes. Any updates will be published on this page.

12. Contact

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact:

infogooweb@gmail.com
